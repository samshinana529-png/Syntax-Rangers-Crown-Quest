Crown Quest

Welcome to Crown Quest! Start your adventure, roll the die, and claim your throne.

  • Roll the die to move your token along the path.
  • Roll a 6 to leave your base and enter the board.
  • Answer trivia after every move to advance or stay safe.

How to Play

Rule Book

Number of Players Supported

Four players are required to play the game at a time. Every player has their own starting point: Player 1, Player 2, Player 3, and Player 4.

Game Components

  • Game Board: Consisting of 4 player stations, pathways in different colours (blue, red, yellow, green, purple, and pink), and a Winner spot in the middle.
  • Six-Faced Die: Black and white
  • Timer: To time the players when answering questions (10 seconds)
  • Trivia Cards: Categorized by colour
  • Player Pawns: Each player has an assigned pawn for gameplay

Gameplay Mechanic During Each Player's Turn

To leave the player box: You need to roll a six to go to the pathway. Every player always starts in a blue tile.

During your turn:

  1. Roll the Die: Wait for it to land at a specific number
  2. Move & Landing Spot: you must roll a four and above to move forward and to answer the question correctly to move forward to the next tile along the pathway. The colour of the tile you land on determines the category of questions you will be asked.
  3. Drawing Cards: Pick a deck of cards depending on the tile colour (for example, if you land on Blue, draw from the Blue "Biology" deck).

Questioning Rules

When rolling a 1, 2, or 3:

  • ✓ Right Answer: Stay where you are. You don't move to the next tile.
  • ✗ Wrong Answer: Go backwards one tile.

When rolling a 4, 5, or 6:

  • ✓ Right Answer: Go forward one tile.
  • ✗ Wrong Answer: No punishment; stay safe where you are.

Time Limit: Each question must be answered within 10 seconds. If time runs out, it counts as a wrong answer.

Winning Criteria

A player wins the game when they reach the yellow "WINNER" square located at the centre of the board after following the guidelines for movement.

Special Rules and Consequences

  • Safe Zone: You can be termed "safe" after rolling a high number (4 or more), as wrong answers have no penalty.
  • The Penalty: There is a significant penalty after rolling a low number (3 or less), because a wrong answer leads to backward motion.
  • Direction Arrows: The direction of the flow of movement as marked by white arrows should be followed in order to reach the middle of the game board.
